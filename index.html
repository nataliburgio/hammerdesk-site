Common questions

What if I don't use all my hours?
Unused hours roll over to the next month (up to 10 hours max). We don't want you paying for time you're not using.
Can I cancel anytime?
Yes. We work month-to-month with 30 days notice. No lock-in contracts, no cancellation fees.
Do you understand construction pricing?
Absolutely. Nat's a licensed builder and we work exclusively with trades. We know the difference between a rough-in and a fix-out, and we price quotes accordingly.
How quickly do you turn around quotes?
Most quotes go out within 24-48 hours. Professional tier gets priority - often same-day for urgent jobs.
What about confidentiality?
We sign NDAs and use secure systems. Your client info and business details stay private.
